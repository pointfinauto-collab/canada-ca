Program Overview

The Field Support Services (FSS) Program is a flagship initiative by Global Affairs Canada designed to strengthen institutional capacity, promote good governance, and advance sustainable development across East Africa and the broader Pan-African region.

Through competitive registration, qualified individuals, organizations, and firms are invited to participate in Canada-funded projects that transform communities and build lasting partnerships between Canada and Africa.

Eligibility Requirements

Individuals

  • Valid passport from eligible country
  • Minimum 5 years relevant experience
  • Academic qualifications in relevant field
  • No criminal record

Organizations

  • Legally registered entity
  • Minimum 3 years operational history
  • Financial statements (last 2 years)
  • Tax compliance certificate

Eligible Countries

  • Ethiopia
  • Sudan
  • South Sudan
  • Canada & partner nations

Frequently Asked Questions
What is the Field Support Services (FSS) Program?⌄
The FSS Program is a Government of Canada initiative managed by Global Affairs Canada. It establishes a roster of pre-qualified individuals, organizations, and firms to deliver technical assistance in Canadian-funded development projects across East Africa.
What is a UIC Number and what is it used for?⌄
A Unique Identification Code (UIC) is automatically generated upon successful registration in the format UIC-CA-2026-XXXXXX. It is used to track your application and in all official correspondence.
How long does the review process take?⌄
Once all required documents are submitted and fees paid, reviews typically take 15-30 business days. Complex applications may require additional information.
What documents are required for registration?⌄
Valid passport copy, updated CV/Resume, relevant academic certificates, and for organizations: company registration certificate, tax compliance certificate, and audited financial statements.
Is there an application fee?⌄
Application fees are assigned individually based on applicant category. Fees are displayed in your dashboard once documents are reviewed.
